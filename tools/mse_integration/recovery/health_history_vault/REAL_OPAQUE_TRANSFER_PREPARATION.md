# Real native Health archive transfer preparation

Only the authorized one-time opaque archive lane. Local sealing, VM intake, and typed parsing are separate gates. This procedure does not release a route or grant. No Health export names, sizes, digests, enrollment IDs, or metadata belong in shared records. The selected pilot and its cutoff remain independent.

## Concrete local prerequisites

The custodian's guarded local receipt must match the exact closed ZIP on the mounted, dedicated AES-256 capture image. The source is regular, owned, single-link and mode 0600, and the private identity volume is detached. Preserve the original local copy. The receiver's limit is 8 GiB per opaque object and 12 GiB maximum history allocation. The current Mac test applies a conservative envelope allowance of 1% plus 1 MiB, then reserves 1 GiB free on both the capture volume and its FileVault backing volume while retaining the original. The guest must stay above its 16 GiB free-space floor, including the full expected upload and catalog overhead. Recheck both systems at the transfer boundary. Hold for a revised reviewed allocation if any check fails; don't split or trim the original export.

Use the verified Mac age v1.3.2 executable, SHA-256 `dceadbf3efeda083d24cd03f77fda2ede1aede07721cc8ba43dd3c50ab110d6c`, and the separately recorded exact approved public hybrid recipient. The private age identity never goes to the receiver. The [closed-file sealer](seal_closed_history_zip.py) requires a pinned exact original receipt, capture mount and recipient, preserves the original, and writes an exclusive fully synced ciphertext. It only records the raw archive length and digest inside a separately encrypted local integrity manifest. The transfer descriptor has only the ciphertext pin, byte length, public recipient pin and opaque import information. Do not print these file contents.

Run the helper only from the private custodian environment once independent local-only review passes:

```sh
python3 -B seal_closed_history_zip.py "$closed_zip" "$local_custody_receipt" \
  "$local_custody_root" "$verified_age" "$verified_age_sha256"
```

A [synthetic rehearsal](test_seal_closed_history_zip.py) tested the exact helper on the verified encrypted capture mount with a different test recipient. It authenticated the exact round trip in memory, checked the encrypted integrity manifest and separate transfer descriptor, and preserved the source. It did not open, seal or upload the real native export.

1. Preserve the completed synthetic root and its receipt. Use a dedicated non-login account, distinct key/config/service, and guest-terminated private TLS. Never reuse the synthetic grant, initialize the original synthetic root, or stage the private age identity on VM.
2. Derive a fresh import enrollment from the exact sealed transfer descriptor. Pin ciphertext length, digest, recipient fingerprint, one opaque import ID, 12 GiB maximum allocation and 16 GiB guest reserve. Use a finite new upload lease derived from the actual transfer budget, independently of the historical dates in the ZIP. Upload grant ends at or before cutoff. Separate finite receipt and ciphertext-reader grants may outlive it briefly for authorized readback. Disabled staging and verified negative PUT precede release.
3. The scoped history relay accepts only the verified Mac peer and narrowly constrained host verifier when required. Set expiry to the same upload cutoff. Use an explicit CA-file pin with exact hostname and leaf, and verify policy order, denied-source counter, wrong trust/role, synthetic-live isolation and lack of public/SQL exposure. Do not alter global Mac trust or existing phone routes.
4. Send only the immutable ciphertext object in at most 4 MiB chunks on the approved exact 8447 route. Each PUT includes exact Content-Length, inclusive Content-Range and SHA-256 for that chunk. A missing response requires authenticated progress GET on the same opaque import; it is not permission to infer a new offset or start a second import. Resume only the exact expected contiguous range. An exact replay is idempotent; mismatched body or offset is a hold. Do not upload the encrypted local integrity manifest, raw ZIP, source receipt, or age identity.
5. Require the exact witnessed durable ciphertext receipt. Reopen receipt under its dedicated role and independently retrieve/re-prove all ciphertext chunks, size and SHA. Off the VM the custodian verifies detached age authentication by streaming through a hash and requiring successful age exit; the encrypted integrity manifest supplies the exact raw length and digest. A decryption stream is not a receipt until final authentication succeeds. Do not unzip or materialize plaintext for this step. Later normalization and BP coverage need separate verified readiness.
6. At cutoff or explicit early stop, verify that only the named 8447 relay stopped and its listener/sessions/owned firewall table are absent. Verify upload refusal, brief authorized direct readback, and durable retained object/receipt/witness. Keep the original local ZIP, ciphertext custody and receipt. Preserve the running selected 8446 relay, timers, services and exact cutoff.
