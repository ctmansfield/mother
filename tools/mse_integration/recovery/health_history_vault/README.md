Use a separate history enrollment, service identity, state/config/install roots, and a separately scoped endpoint. Reserved names for review are `mse-health-history`, `/var/lib/phoenix-mse-health-history`, `/etc/phoenix-mse-health-history`, and `/opt/phoenix-mse-health-history`. The isolated guest's proposed 8447 bind and these paths were unused at preflight. Keep the two existing service roots and their grants out of the history implementation.

For the first synthetic capacity trial, propose **8 GiB maximum accepted ciphertext**, one in-flight object per enrollment, **12 GiB maximum total history allocation**, and a hard **16 GiB guest free-space floor**. The allocation must include incomplete ciphertext, catalog, and any same-filesystem staging. Publish by atomic rename in the same filesystem and resume one quarantined object by stable import ID rather than admitting a second full-size copy. A private encrypted working volume has a separate admission budget for decoded/walked bytes and typed database scratch. The staged trial values are limits, not estimates of Chad's export. Measure the qualified source byte count and the parser's resource amplification before authorizing real capture. If a complete export cannot fit, hold the import and agree the exact revised allocation. Never trim or silently skip categories to fit a cap.

Do not use iCloud Drive, email, chat, ordinary Downloads/AirDrop, general-purpose temp folders, or an uncontrolled File Provider. Native Health may use OS-managed transient files on the phone. Verify the protection boundary before proceeding. The confirmed Mac FileVault volume is a host capability, not approval for a plaintext inbox.
2. Establish a designated raw-data custodian and a distinct identity-key custody location before a real export. The custodian generates a fresh native `age` hybrid ML-KEM-768 + X25519 recipient on the controlled encrypted key volume with `age-keygen -pq -o "$identity_file"`, then derives a public recipient file with `age-keygen -y -o "$recipient_file" "$identity_file"`. The sole private identity remains mode 0600 under encrypted, restricted custody. The ingest service, relay, projection and Mother/Coach never receive it. Tie the approved public recipient pin to the enrollment and define access and key-loss disposition. This is primary-custody work, not backup qualification.
3. Treat the entire Apple export container as the original. Compute its exact length and SHA-256 in protected custody. Encrypt the exact bytes with `age --encrypt --recipients-file "$recipient_file" -o "$ciphertext" "$native_zip"` without unpacking or rewriting it. Keep the plaintext digest and full member inventory inside the sealed custody manifest. The ingest service needs ciphertext length/SHA-256, an opaque enrollment/import identity, and its bounded upload grant. The vault may retain ciphertext on guest ext4 because the decryption key lives elsewhere. Host administrators can still observe ciphertext, traffic timing and exposed catalog metadata. No claim of protection while a custodian decrypts or about production anonymity.
4. Keep the raw vault key separate from the pseudonym mapping/HMAC key, TLS private key, import bearer, and typed/operator credentials. The consumer projection must never gain a decrypt capability. Authorized analysis opens complete authenticated ciphertext in a restricted encrypted work volume or controlled streaming path. A plaintext ZIP may not be materialized in an unprotected directory. Require successful final authentication before a typed import can publish. Retention and explicit erase are distinct policy decisions.

The synthetic rehearsal uses exact `age v1.3.2` from the [Homebrew package](https://formulae.brew.sh/formula/age), using its arm64 Golden Gate bottle SHA-256 `ab6acf805fc7c3bd78da358bd3e4b3d4df3cabf6d070e5cc86745643012a4ee0`. ## Coverage and consumer boundary

Raw retention is not typed availability. The protected inventory records every original container member and attachment, exact hashes, declared and observed structural limits, and per-family available/parsed/normalized/rejected counts and time ranges. ATLAS and FORGE own family and version mappings, bounded ZIP/XML parsing, exact source clocks, deduplication, and live-overlap revision semantics. Treat paths, arbitrary metadata, routes and attachments as restricted. Reject path escape, duplicate/oversized entries, ZIP bombs, unsafe XML features, and incomplete inputs. BP acceptance requires source availability and paired systolic/diastolic lineage, with unpaired states preserved and cuff pulse separate unless linked. No health values go in release reports.

Use a separate purpose-scoped pseudonymous projection for Mother/Coach. Its read-only role should receive approved BP/SGV fields and provenance, with no raw source or recipient key access, no identity mapping, device identifiers or location. Keep approved time resolution purpose-bound; time and physiology may identify someone even when direct identifiers are removed. A direct Nightscout reader in Mother/Coach would expose a source credential and provider schema to the consumer boundary. The safer candidate is a separately authorized scoped ingestion lane feeding a pseudonymous read-only store. Existing [Nightscout N0 evidence](https://github.com/ctmansfield/mother/issues/10#issuecomment-6857127042) documents one bounded historical pilot, not continuous access, full backfill or qualified units. New real gateway access, credential polling, vendor changes, and deployment remain out of this source-only review.
