# Native Health ZIP universal review candidate

Scope is MSE-ALL-ZIP-DATA-20261121-07 revision 2. Every real-data
step is user-manual, including offline preflight, admissions, verification
and cleanup. Preserve the exact
original and the successful PR #61 inventory and BP catalog. Use a
fresh encrypted catalog if this candidate is ever admitted. No agent
runs the real archive. This document is for independent source and
environment qualification.

## Protected catalog

Every admitted member, including directories, is represented in the
member ledger. Files, including empty files and unsupported binary
formats, have exact expanded-byte retrieval by member and digest. SQLite
stores their data in verified 64 KiB chunks and keeps distinct member
links for duplicate payloads. Raw ZIP container bytes remain in original
custody. The source role receives a bounded response under the exact
import, member ordinal and expected digest, never from a source path.

HealthData and selected safe XML/GPX have ordered document events and
indexed preorder elements with parents, physical child positions,
qualified names, namespaces and ordered attributes. Text, mixed content,
comments, declarations and processing instructions remain in protected
storage. Exact expanded member bytes preserve lexical fidelity.
References stay inert. Exact archive-local links resolve to an existing
member ordinal; external, escaping and unresolved references do not
produce network or filesystem access. Parser and integrity refusals
roll back the entire native transaction. Unsupported opaque formats
retain exact byte access.

Top-level records have one primary typed, fallback or quarantine
disposition. Elements and content events have separate denominators.
Initial typed adapters cover exact quantity decimals and units, category
and sleep source codes, workout descriptors, activity dates,
characteristics codes, explicit correlations, BP, and limited descriptors
for some specialized families. Where signal, dose, clinical, vision, or
other family semantics are unqualified, the source representation is
retrievable. An opaque payload or descriptor is not normalized physiology.

## Manual Mac output and finite guest transfer

The signed Mac policy may carry a `universal_transfer` object with a new
protected `output` path, an opaque `import_ref`, and bounded `max_bytes`.
It keeps the signed ZIP member allowance and all other admission checks.
The admitted Mac entry point authenticates the original before parsing
and reopens its committed receipt. On successful output publication the
fresh directory contains `exact-source.frames` and a
`private-manifest.json`. Keep both private. Preserve a committed catalog
if the spool fails and reconcile before authorizing anything further.

The independent guest policy pins the exact transfer manifest and the
LUKS2 mapping, database cap, reserve, private TLS certificate and CA
digests, exact scope, and finite distinct writer, receipt and restricted
source roles. Each Mother/Coach grant has a separate subject and versioned
allowlists for approved adapters, source types, units, coded values and
activity codes, plus fixed event interval, timeless-record scope and row
limit. Default purposes have no raw member, source-tree or receipt
access. The restricted source role has access only under the admitted
artifact. A full catalog or unrestricted set of records must not be sent
to a model. Verify actual encrypted storage and least privilege on the
target guest before enabling any route.

The guest entry point is `native_universal_guest`. The independently
trusted SHA-256 pins the private admission file and existing operator
provisioning. Initialization creates a fresh database. Serving is a
separate operator action. Mac `native_universal_client preflight` verifies
the exact protected spool and makes no network request. The separate
`execute-manual` mode transfers fixed 512 KiB parts using hostname-verified
mTLS with a writer identity and independently verifies the witnessed
terminal receipt with the receipt identity. `readback` verifies the same
receipt after a lost response. Do not automatically retry refused or
indeterminate outcomes. Keep the exact staged bytes and record sequence
until an authorized reconciliation.

The guest rehashes full member contents and checks integrity links and
structural totals before staging a terminal result. A trusted write-window
observation after synchronous commit is persisted separately as the
publication witness; an unfinished decision stays sealed on reopen.
Neither default purpose nor restricted reads can see staged contents.
No backup/restore, live collector, or Nightscout work is included.

## Review gates

Fabricated local verification covers mixed XML, unknown source trees,
named quarantine, exact blob and range reads, duplicate and empty
members, deterministic replay, cutoff without a terminal witness,
receipt reopen, loopback mTLS, scope denial, and separate purpose views.
Independent reviewers must reconcile the full fixture ledger, validate
additional family adapters and reader contracts, and qualify actual
protected persistent storage and transport on the target guest. NICKY
then owns the exact-pinned user-manual launcher. This source package alone
does not qualify a real archive run or real-data transfer.

## Reviewed access and reconciliation profile

Every accepted parser event is reconciled against the member, element
stack, physical parent/child ordering, top-level occurrence and stored
reference status before terminal publication. The protected source
coverage response reports top-level dispositions and nested family/type
counts separately. A ZIP member must remain retrievable even when the
structured parser has no qualified decoder. Unknown and quarantine rows
retain their exact source events and payload in the restricted source
role. Source reference pages expose exact stored resolved, unresolved,
or blocked dispositions without fetching paths or URLs.

| Family | Qualified queryable fields in this candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Quantities | Canonical decimal, exposed source decimal and unit, and parseable source/UTC clocks |
| Categories and sleep | Exposed coded state and parseable source/UTC clocks |
| BP | Explicit correlated or standalone source facts, source and canonical values, clocks, and physical child lineage |
| Workouts | Activity code, parseable clocks, and qualified duration and its source unit when supplied |
| Activity | Qualified local date when supplied |
| Characteristics | Selected explicitly recognized source codes |
| Other correlations | Partial descriptor and exact structured membership |
| Routes, heartbeat series, ECG, clinical, medication, vision and audiogram | Partial descriptor fields when recognized and exact source tree or member payload. Waveforms, geometry, clinical semantics, dose schedules and other unqualified fields remain fallback |
| Unknown and auxiliary XML | Exact member bytes and ordered structured fallback when safely parsed |
| Other attachments | Exact member payload and integrity-bound range retrieval |

The top-level denominator is typed + descriptor + fallback + quarantine.
Element and nested-family counts are independent. Mother/Coach feature
discovery reports approved projected fields and scope. The source role
has a separate observed coverage endpoint. Default purpose pages order
by eligible event clock and a keyed opaque tie-breaker. An authenticated
cursor binds the accepted import and purpose policy without encoding
physical member or ordinal values.

The guest admission includes a private 32-byte `cursor_key_file` and its
independently pinned `cursor_key_sha256`. It also includes
`reviewed_reads`, a map keyed by individually approved certificate-grant
role. Each role binds one purpose and audience with one fallback element
and exact attributes, or one exact member/digest and permitted range plus
total byte budget. Use a distinct certificate with an explicit expiry
for each. A reviewed fallback grant cannot read quarantine. Approved
attachment bytes are charged in the protected database before response;
reopen does not reset the budget. Default purpose roles cannot use these
paths. A policy that needs no individual grants uses an empty map.

Budget the guest for two signed catalog caps and the reviewed free-space
reserve on the approved encrypted filesystem, and verify backing
capacity separately. The stored catalog cap must account for both the
retained wire parts and materialized records/chunks. An earlier local
fabricated 9 MiB case needed about 12.06 MiB of spool, 12.25 MiB of staged
SQLite, and 21.40 MiB of witnessed SQLite. Those measurements inform the
operator estimate. They do not establish a production bound or certify the
target volume.

Purpose discovery reports whether genuinely timeless rows are eligible.
HealthData occurrences with an invalid supplied clock, a reversed interval,
or an invalid ActivitySummary calendar date are quarantined with a reason.
A malformed BP root or required component makes the whole occurrence
ineligible. Quarantine keeps exact source events and member bytes. Purpose
reads also exclude reversed intervals in earlier typed data. Source member
discovery reports a byte-only disposition and a reason for attachments,
including GPX unreadable as supported XML.
Default purpose cursors refer to the last returned eligible row and are
present only when another eligible row exists. Restart pagination after
upgrading from the previous candidate. Reviewed fallback grants may
select exact attributes of a nested element under a typed occurrence.
Approved source XML also includes UTF-16 GPX recognized by a byte-order
mark or XML opening signature. The parser retains the original member
bytes and indexes decoded structure independently.
